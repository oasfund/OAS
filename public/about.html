About OAS

The Open Amount Sinking Fund (OAS) Management System is a web-based platform designed to manage member contributions, loan applications, collections, referral incentives, and administrative operations in one centralized system.

Mission

To provide a simple, transparent, and reliable financial assistance program for members and qualified borrowers.

Vision

To become a trusted community-based financial management platform through innovation, accountability, and sustainable growth.
