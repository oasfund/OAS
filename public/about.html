About OAS

The Open Amount Sinking Fund (OAS) Management System is designed to manage member registration, loan applications, weekly contributions, referral incentives, and administrative reporting through a simple and organized web platform.

Mission

To provide an organized, transparent, and reliable financial management system for all OAS members and borrowers.

Vision

To become a trusted and efficient digital management platform for the OAS community.
